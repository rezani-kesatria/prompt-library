( tenon.studio )

We are a furniture workshop making seating, tables and casegoods in small runs — cut, fitted and pegged by hand, in timber selected board by board.

T

Seating

Chairs, benches and stools. Steam-bent and hand-shaped, joined to hold a century of sitting.

Tables & casegoods

Dining, writing and storage. Solid tops with breadboard ends that let the timber move.

Commission

One-off pieces drawn with architects and interior designers, from survey to installation.

Restoration

Structural repair and refinishing of joinery worth keeping, in the methods it was built with.

( the workshop )

A former grain store, six makers and a timber rack that runs two years ahead of what we build.

We buy standing timber and mill it ourselves, then let it sit. Nothing leaves the bench until the moisture content matches the room it is going to. That is most of the reason a piece takes eleven weeks instead of three.
