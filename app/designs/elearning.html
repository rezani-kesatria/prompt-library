FAQ

Is the app suitable for beginners?

Absolutely. Every path starts from first principles — no prior knowledge of art history is assumed, and lessons build gradually.

Can I use the app offline?

You can download lessons and quizzes for offline use. Your progress syncs automatically the next time you reconnect.

What topics do you cover?

From the Renaissance to Ukiyo-e, Impressionism, Symbolism and contemporary practice — plus technique, colour theory and composition.

Is my progress synced across devices?

Yes. Start a lesson on your phone and pick it up on the web exactly where you left off.

Are there free lessons available?

The first lesson of every course is free, along with a rotating selection of quizzes.

How often is new content added?

We publish a new course each month and add fresh quizzes every week.
